Executive snapshot

MyEventScape.com unifies customers, vendors, public safety, and city officials so every parade, street fair, and race is organized with confidence. The platform layers a high-converting marketing story over a secure paywalled dashboard that hosts permits, maps, inbox threads, and an AI co-pilot.

  • Supabase backend & auth
  • Stripe paywall + role gating
  • Google Maps Studio with vendor metadata
  • AI guidance referencing city rule packs

Layered home experience

"Now" showcases the promise. "Next" gives paying planners superpowers.

The home experience is intentionally split: the public layer (Now) nurtures prospects and routes them to authentication, while the private layer (Next) delivers the AI-powered dashboard tailored to each role.

Public marketing surface

Hero storytelling, proof points, pricing tiers, testimonials, and FAQ previews educate prospective planners before they create an account. Prominent CTAs route to the Supabase Auth-backed paywall, blending trust and urgency.

  • Feature highlight cards tied to permit speed, AI compliance, and live collaboration.
  • Knowledge base teasers, footer with privacy/terms, and inbound trial signup flow.
  • Conversion goal: 6% trial start rate across campaigns.

This layer speaks to the Prospective Planner Visitor persona. Once subscribed, visitors land inside the secure dashboard layer below.

Paywalled dashboard

After subscription, users access modules like Permit Center, Event Manager, Map Studio, Vendor Portal, Inbox, and AI Guidance. Role-based access keeps planners, vendors, public safety, and city reviewers focused on the right data.

  • Guided permit builder with AI pre-checks and attachment uploads.
  • Google Maps drawing tools with layers for vendors, utilities, emergency zones.
  • Messaging inbox with tagging, attachments, and notifications.
  • Vendor onboarding portal with booth assignments and chat.

Stripe (or similar) enforces subscription status before Supabase delivers session tokens and role permissions for Planner Admin, Vendor, Public Safety, and City Reviewer roles.

Data backbone

Supabase models keep every stakeholder in sync.

Core tables

  • organizations (tier, settings)
  • users (profile, role, organization_id)
  • events (metadata, schedule, status)
  • permits (type, form_payload, status, reviewer_notes)
  • maps (event_id, geojson, version metadata)

Collaboration tables

  • vendor_assignments (booth details)
  • messages (threads, recipients, attachments)
  • notifications (payload, read state)

Intelligence tables

  • ai_guidelines (city rules, last updated)
  • audit logs for compliance
  • Edge functions call OpenAI for scoring & suggestions

Open questions & risks

Areas to monitor as layers evolve.

City-specific onboarding

Need admin tooling to seed municipal rules quickly.

Submission requirements

Some cities may still require PDF exports; fallback workflows needed.

Data sensitivity

Safety plans may require extra encryption & key management.

Google Maps cost

High draw usage could drive API costs—need monitoring & caching.

Pricing strategy

Balance SMB planners vs. enterprise cities to hit revenue goals.
